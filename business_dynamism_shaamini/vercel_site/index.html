Companies House · density

Company density on a fixed grid

A choropleth’s colour depends partly on the size of the boundary it fills, which flatters large rural authorities and dilutes dense small ones. On a fixed grid of cells every unit is the same size on the ground, so the pattern follows where companies are registered rather than how the administrative map is drawn. cells are occupied; the densest holds registrations, and the busiest tenth of cells accounts for of the register.

Each cell is coloured by the number of companies whose registered postcode falls inside it. Cell size is uniform in projected space, so no boundary geography enters the picture.

Survival by birth cohort

Kaplan-Meier estimates for cohorts, right-censored at the last complete Gazette year; each curve ends at the oldest age its whole cohort has been observed to reach, at most years. Solid lines count insolvency only; dashed lines count any exit — insolvency plus solvent liquidation and dissolution — so the gap between a cohort’s two lines is the share that left the register without failing.

Later cohorts sit above earlier ones, but this should not yet be read as companies surviving longer. The register snapshot carries no dissolution record at all: of companies with a dated Gazette dissolution are absent from it, and the shortfall is largest for the oldest dissolutions. Part of the apparent improvement is therefore missing deaths rather than longer lives; the Companies House extraction described at the foot of this page is what will separate the two.

Forthcoming · declining business dynamism

Entry rates and the age structure of the population

Whether entry rates are falling and the firm population ageing, as documented for the United States, requires knowing which companies are still trading. The dissolution record is incomplete: dissolved companies are not always marked as such, and the earliest years under-observe long-lived firms.

An extraction from the Companies House API is addressing this, recovering incorporation date, dissolution date, status and industry for every dissolved or former company. company profiles have been retrieved, carrying a dissolution date.
