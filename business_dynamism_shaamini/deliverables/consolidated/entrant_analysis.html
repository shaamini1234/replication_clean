Are "new" index members genuinely new companies?

First-time entrants to the FTSE 100 & S&P 500 by 5-year window. The youngest-at-entry cases were adjudicated against sources; each carries a source URL in the classified CSVs.

Headline. Almost every entrant that looks new is an established business arriving via a corporate action — a spin-off, merger, demutualisation or reincorporation — not a genuinely new company. Among the youngest-at-entry cohort, the S&P amber cases were 100% corporate actions (18 spin-offs, 8 mergers, 2 renames; zero new businesses); on the FTSE side only Energis was a true new-business entrant.
