For whoever picks this up next. Read this before touching the data.

39M-row Companies House firm-year panel, plus FTSE 100 and S&P 500 index

## Things that will bite you

**Join on `cik` (S&P) or `company_number` (FTSE). Never ticker.** Tickers are
reused after a delisting. Seven S&P symbols pointed at the wrong company —
Compuware was recorded as a utility because `sp500_companies_full` resolved
tickers against a present-day lookup. On the FTSE side `RSA` maps to three
different companies. A ticker may be used to LOOK UP an identifier via
`sp500_identity`; never as the join key.

**Read identifiers as strings.** `pd.read_csv` turns `0000773910` into `773910`
and the join then silently matches nothing. Pass
`dtype={"cik": str, "company_number": str}`.

**Collapse to one row per identifier before merging.** One company can hold
several tickers (GOOG/GOOGL, FB/META, BSY/SKY); an uncollapsed CIK merge
multiplies rows. `build_consolidated_csvs.py` has a row-count assertion for this
because it happened.

**Materialised tables drift.** `ftse100_consolidated` and `sp500_consolidated`
exist as both CSVs and DuckDB tables. The site reads the tables.
`build_consolidated_csvs.py` now refreshes both together — do not rebuild only
the CSVs.

**DuckDB `UPDATE` on 39M rows grinds.** It is columnar and rewrites row groups.
Use `CREATE TABLE AS SELECT`. In Postgres, never backfill a column across all
rows — use `DEFAULT`; a full-table UPDATE holds an exclusive lock for minutes.

**`psql -tAc` mangles multi-line SQL.** It matched zero rows while reporting
success. Pipe on stdin with `ON_ERROR_STOP=1`.

## Data quality: what is fixed, what is not

Fixed and verified by query:
- CIK is `VARCHAR(10)` across all 7 S&P tables; 0 remaining source conflicts.
- Sector on `sp500_consolidated`: 1,048 of 1,049 symbols (was 499). Exited-member
  coverage 27% -> 99.8%, removing a survivorship bias inherited from the
  Wikipedia current-constituent list.
- The site build is deterministic — all five map files byte-identical across
  runs; `site.json` varies only by its build timestamp.
- Three false filings (PARAGLIDE GBP 540bn, PETROMARUZ GBP 191bn, AVANTULO
  GBP 26bn) are flagged AND their monetary values nulled. They were 28% of the
  GVA total. GVA now reads GBP 326.3bn with no filter needed.
- 9.4M impossible values nulled (negative employees, negative total assets).
  Every original is preserved in `data_quality_corrections`.
- FTSE membership: every row has a company number or an explicit reason it has
  none. `unknown_age` entrants 107 -> 0.

Not fixed, and important:
- **Sector is ~87% CORRECT, not 99.9%.** Coverage is 99.9%; the SIC->GICS
  mapping agrees with real GICS 86.7% of the time. `sector_basis` says which
  rows are `index_list_gics` (authoritative) versus mapped. Do not conflate
  coverage with correctness.
- **69 FTSE constituents were incorporated AFTER the date they entered the
  index.** Flagged via `ch_match_status`. The assumption is that the company
  number is wrong; it could equally be the entry date. Unverified.
- **66 historical FTSE names have no company number.** A Companies House name
  search returns confident-looking wrong matches ("Eastern Electricity" matches a
  2023 incorporation), so they are deliberately left null.
- **110 S&P symbols have no CIK.** Kept under a `TICKER:` key, never merged.
- `size_class` is unreliable on 4,725 firm-years (`large` on micro-entity
  filings). Any size-banded cut inherits this.

## Structural limits — cannot be fixed, must be stated

**Turnover exists for 2.09% of firm-years.** UK law exempts small and micro
companies from filing a profit and loss account. Validated against ONS Annual
Business Survey: the panel captures 3.84% of UK turnover overall, under 1.5%
before 2017. Any revenue time series from this data shows filing-coverage
improvement, not economic growth. Under ECCTA small companies must file a P&L
from April 2028, but may opt out of publication — so this will not fully resolve.

GVA is computable on 60,719 filings (0.15%). The binding constraint is
`operating_profit` at 0.93%, not depreciation.

For whole-economy value added use ONS ABS aGVA (GBP 1,735bn UK 2024, free, OGL
v3.0, SIC x region x year 2008-2024). Do not estimate up from company filings:
the turnover/assets ratio has a 650x p10-p90 spread and sector-level aggregates
come out +62%/-24% wrong.

## Open decisions — for the user, not the agent

1. **What counts as "tech".** Information Technology alone gives 1 genuinely-new
   FTSE entrant ever (Sage); adding Communication Services gives 4. Changes the
   headline.
2. **The `re_entry` rule.** It is set from a company's TOTAL episode count, so a
   company whose FIRST entry was genuinely new is labelled `re_entry` because it
   later returned. Contradicts the classifier's own first-time-entrant framing.
   11 rows affected, including Darktrace (entered 2021 aged 8). Changing it moves
   them to the amber bucket for adjudication, not straight to genuinely-new.
